## Expected Approach
1. **Web search** — tavily/searxng for "Eliot Higgins" to establish baseline
2. **Username enumeration** — Check for known handles across platforms
3. **Domain search** — Check bellingcat.com WHOIS, DNS, infrastructure
4. **Social media** — Twitter/X (@EliotHiggins is well-known), LinkedIn, etc.
5. **Knowledge graph** — Map person → organization → domains → social accounts

## Verification
- Eliot Higgins is a public figure, founder of Bellingcat
- His Twitter/X handle is publicly known (@EliotHiggins)
- bellingcat.com is the organization's domain
- Multiple public interviews, talks, and publications exist
